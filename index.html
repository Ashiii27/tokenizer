How LLMs read text

Two mini-demos that run real transformer models entirely in your browser — no server, no setup. On first visit the models download from the Hugging Face Hub; after that they come from your browser cache.

1 Tokenization Xenova/gpt2

A language model never sees letters or words — a tokenizer first splits text into small pieces called tokens (often whole words or word fragments) and maps each piece to an ID number from a fixed vocabulary, and those IDs are the only thing the model actually reads.

2 Embeddings & similarity Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2

An embedding model compresses a whole text into a single vector — 384 numbers that capture its meaning — so comparing two vectors with cosine similarity (a dot product of the two normalized vectors) gives a score for how close in meaning the texts are.
