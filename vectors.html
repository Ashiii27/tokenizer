§ 04 — meaning as numbers

Vectors & cosine all-MiniLM-L6-v2 · 384d

An embedding model squeezes a whole sentence into 384 numbers. Similar meanings produce similar numbers, so the angle between two vectors — cosine similarity — becomes a measure of how close in meaning two texts are.

Try this

  1. Press a tricky pair. Hot and cold score high, because opposites live in the same contexts.
  2. Try river bank against bank account. One vector cannot hold two meanings.
  3. Read the twelve bars under the score — those are the dimensions that actually moved it.

What drives that score

The dot product is a sum of Ai × Bi across all 384 dimensions. These are the twelve that contribute most — positive bars pull the texts together, negative bars push them apart.

What you’re looking at

  • The first comparison downloads about 23 MB of model. It is cached afterwards, and nothing you type is uploaded.
  • Scores are only comparable inside one model. 0.6 means nothing across two different embedding models.
  • MiniLM reads 512 tokens and silently ignores the rest — the pill above the score warns you when that happened.

Everything here runs locally.
